Administrator's Message

Sr. Naveena - Administrator, Carmel Hospital Aluva

Dear beloved patients, families and community members,

As we strive to provide compassionate care to those in need we are reminded of Jesus' words "Love your neighbor as yourself." (Mk 12:31)

At Carmel Hospital we are committed to living out this commandment by serving our community with love, kindness, and respect. Our dedicated team of healthcare professionals and support staff are called to care for the whole person—body, mind, and spirit.

We believe that every individual who walks through our doors is a precious child of God, deserving of dignity, compassion, and exceptional care. We are honored to serve you and provide a safe, supportive environment for healing and recovery.

As we move ahead, we pray for God's guidance, wisdom, and blessing on our efforts. May our hospital be a beacon of hope, a refuge for the hurting, and a testament to the transformative power of love and compassion.

Thank you for entrusting us with your care. We're privileged to serve you. May God bless and guide us all.

We Care About You

Embrace your Health

We are committed to providing exceptional healthcare services to all our patients. Our team of healthcare professionals comprises experienced and skilled doctors, nurses, and other medical staff who are dedicated to ensuring your well-being. We understand that each patient is unique, and we provide personalized care to meet their individual needs.

Primary Health Care

We understand the importance of primary health care in maintaining good health and preventing illnesses. Our team of healthcare professionals provides comprehensive primary care services including regular check-ups, screenings, immunizations, and chronic disease management to help you stay healthy.

Achieving Better Health Care

Our team is committed to providing you with the best possible care to help you achieve your health goals. We provide comprehensive medical services that are focused on preventing illness, managing chronic conditions, and promoting overall health and well-being with personalized treatment plans.
